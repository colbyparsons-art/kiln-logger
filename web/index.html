        Alert and firing settings

        People who can get alerts

        Use a nickname or initials, never a student's full name. Each person gets a PIN and a private alert channel. A check under Can sign up only lets them sign up for alerts on firings; it doesn't send them every alert. Uncheck someone to block them and stop any alerts they're signed up for.

        How phone alerts work

        1. The moderator adds each person here and emails them an invite with their setup steps and PIN.
        2. The person installs the free ntfy app and subscribes to their own channel (one time).
        3. When they fire, they tap Get alerts for this firing on the Live page and enter their PIN. They can sign up before lighting, too.
        4. Only people signed up for a firing get its alerts. The moderator gets the alerts when nobody signs up.
