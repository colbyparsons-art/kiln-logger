        Alert and firing settings

        People who can get alerts

        Use a nickname or initials, never a student's full name. Each person gets a PIN and a private alert channel. Switch someone off to stop all their alerts.

        How phone alerts work

        1. The department head adds each person here and shares their phone setup code and PIN with them.
        2. The person installs the free ntfy app and subscribes to their own channel (one time).
        3. When they fire, they tap Get alerts for this firing on the Live page and enter their PIN. They can sign up before lighting, too.
        4. Only people signed up for a firing get its alerts. The department head is the fallback when nobody signs up.
