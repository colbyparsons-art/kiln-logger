        Getting alerts on your phone

        1. Install the free ntfy app (iPhone or Android).
        2. Tap + and subscribe to the kiln's alert topic. Ask the department head for the topic name; anyone who knows it can subscribe, so share it only with people who should get alerts.
        3. Alerts arrive for: firing started/complete, over temperature, stalled climb, thermocouple fault, and logger offline.
